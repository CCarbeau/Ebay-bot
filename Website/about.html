Subscription Value

We Value Transparency:

We value transparency and think it improves the customer's experience. As a result, we want to publish how we determined our subcriptions values. We strive to provide a service worth more than its price because we want you to use it to make money.

Determining Value:

We ran our service for a week and compiled all of the emails it sent out. We then used the process shown above to determine the amount of value generated for every card in our emails. The total value generated for the week was $______. Multiplying that number by 30/7, we estimated a monthly generated value of $______. Acknowledging that one person will not win every single auction, we divided that total monthly value by 100, estimating that each user receives 1% of the total value of the algorithm. Finally, we arrive at our monthly subscription value of $______.
