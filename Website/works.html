HOW IT WORKS

The Casual Subscription Email

Once you join, you will be sent an email every 2 hours that resembles the one shown above. Each auction target is an item ending within the next 2 hours that we determined as underpriced. The list of targets is sorted from most to least discounted. The link column on the far right sends you directly to the eBay auction page. See the target cards page to see what cards we track.

NOTE: you should not buy every identified target. Some cards are mislabeled and give the card improper features. For example, some cards will say they are a Bowman 1st Auto but will be in-person signed cards. We try to eliminate these mislabeled targets as much as possible, but sometimes they are labelled exactly as the targeted card would be so they slip through our filters.

The Pro Subscription Email

The Pro Level Email has the same frequency, tracked cards, and auction targets as the Casual Level Email but also includes Best Offer and Buy Now Targets. These are items that we think are listed at the same or lower prices than their market value. The Best Offer targets will allow you to get a great deal by offering lower prices than the already low buy now price. The Buy Now targets are items that are listed for significantly less than the next highest buy now item for that card. THERE ARE NO DUPLICATES BETWEEN THE BEST OFFER AND BUY NOW TABLES. The images above show what these sections look like. The tables for each will be added to the bottom of the email, you will not receive multiple emails at one time.
